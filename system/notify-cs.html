Counter · Veteran Exemption

CS01
  • No certificate, asking about eligibility. Refer to Veterans Services Department. The Assessor counter does not determine eligibility; we apply the exemption only once the certificate is in hand. CS01 · Out of scope
  • Certificate looks altered or photocopied. Do not endorse. Hold the parcel, log the visit, and escalate to the supervisor before any IASWorld entry. CS01 · Verify step
  • Applicant status conflict. Applicant claims first-time (F) but IASWorld shows prior exemption — pause, confirm, and escalate if mismatch persists. CS01 · User Fields panel
  • Removing an exemption (REXM). Note REXM with the date, reason, and supervisor sign-off recorded in the case notes. CS01 · Removal path

Phone system · Sensitive edits

CS02 → CS08
  • Any RingCentral edit without a pre-edit snapshot. Universal rule — screenshot the current state of the screen before changing anything. Save it with the change-log entry. CS02 universal · prerequisite for CS03–CS08
  • Adding or removing a queue member during business hours. Roster changes affect routing immediately. Notify the queue manager before the change. CS03 · Members
  • Changing the queue's routing method. Rotating / fixed / longest-idle / simultaneous / group — every method changes who picks up. Coordinate with the queue manager and notify staff. CS04 · Routing
  • Office Closed snow-day toggle. Verify with the Deputy or Assessor before flipping. Affects every inbound caller. CS05 · Custom rules
  • Identity, PIN, extension, or DID change. Heavy consequences — voicemail re-routing, lost direct lines. Notify the affected user and IT before saving. CS07 · Identity
  • Whitelisted-domain or blocked-number changes. Confirm with office IT before applying. Can silently drop legitimate callers. CS06 · Blocked calls · CS08 · DID routing

Reporting · Envoy data

CS09
  • Unfiltered export from the Alvarado account. The account is shared with the entire county. Filtering to Assessor visitor types must happen before export. An unfiltered export is a data spill — stop, delete, re-do. CS09 · Filter first
  • External data request. Records requests or third-party hand-offs do not go straight from Envoy to email. Route through the records-request process — AIO07 if the request touches A.C.E. records. CS09 · External hand-off · see also AIO07
  • Microsoft SSO sign-in failure. Notify office IT before workarounds. Do not export from a personal account. CS09 · Sign-in
  • Row count looks implausible after a filter. Stop. The filter is wrong or the date range is off — verify before exporting and hand-off. CS09 · Verify

LEAP · District Court

CS10 · CS11
  • Physical packet chain of custody broken. The District Court packet is evidence. Never on an open counter. Never in an unlocked drawer. If the chain is broken, document immediately and notify the supervisor. CS11 · Physical handoff (step 14)
  • Wrong parcel in the packet. Do not create the AA record. Log it, return the packet to District Court, and notify Operational Services. CS11 · Validate
  • Hearings page blank. Sub-procedure CS10 — run it before continuing CS11. CS10 · Missing AA Record
  • Missing or unreadable claim amount. Return packet to District Court — do not estimate. Document the date and reason. CS11 · Notes
  • Stipulated Final Judgement signature missing. Hold the AA record at Draft. Email Operational Services for clarification. CS11 · Status

Cross-cutting · PII & safety

All CS SOPs
  • Caller reveals PII unprompted. SSN, full bank or tax-return numbers, medical details. Stop, do not transcribe, and steer the caller to the secure channel for the topic at hand. All CS SOPs
  • Threat against staff or facility. Notify the supervisor and County security within 1 hour with the timestamp, channel, caller info if known, and exact wording. All CS SOPs · mirrors AIO05 Type D
  • Caller asking about valuation methodology, protests, or litigation. Refer to the appropriate division — Counter staff do not advise on protest outcomes or litigation. All CS SOPs · Out of scope
  • Caller asking about another county. Refer to that county's Assessor. We answer only for Bernalillo County. All CS SOPs
  • Repeat or pattern caller showing escalating frustration. Flag to the supervisor before the next interaction — pre-empt rather than wait for a complaint. All CS SOPs

A.C.E. forwarding & AI handoffs

CS05 · CS06 · interplay with AIO
  • A.C.E. chatbot forwarding rule change. Coordinates with the AIO track — notify the AI Project Lead before flipping the rule. Caller experience changes immediately. CS05 · A.C.E. rule
  • Caller reports A.C.E. said something wrong or sensitive. Open AIO05 (incident response) — contain before notifying. Do not promise a fix or discuss specifics with the caller. CS phone scripts · see also AIO05

How to notify

Email the supervisor and the affected queue manager on the same thread. Use a subject line that names the SOP and the severity word.

Subject: CS09 / CRITICAL — short description

For Critical items (PII, threats, broken chain-of-custody, unfiltered Alvarado export), also send a phone or chat ping in addition to the email. Don't wait for a reply before containment.

What the email should include

Five lines, in this order. Keep it tight — detail goes in the case note or incident log.

  • What happened — one sentence.
  • When — timestamp.
  • Scope — who is affected.
  • What you've done — containment, return, hold, snapshot.
  • What you need from them — acknowledge, decision, or just FYI.
Refer ≠ Notify ≠ Hand-off. A referral sends the caller to another office or division (Veterans Services, another county, a different division here). A notify keeps the work at our counter and tells a lead or office IT what's happening. A hand-off physically moves a packet or a record — chain of custody applies. When in doubt, document the choice you made.
