When to notify the Assessor & Deputy

Every same-day escalation trigger across the AI Operations SOPs, on one page. Print and post by your desk. If you see any of the conditions below — send the notification today, then follow the linked SOP.

Daily check

AIO01
  • PII produced or exposed. Any prompt contains personally identifiable information that A.C.E. produced or revealed. AIO01 · Contain first per AIO05 Type C
  • Threats against staff or office. Any prompt with threatening language toward people or the building. AIO01 · Notify County security per AIO05 Type D
  • Persona Failure elevated. Persona Failure incidents on the day are clearly above the 7-day pattern. AIO01 · Investigate per AIO05 Type A
  • Agent unreachable. A.C.E. is unreachable from the public site. AIO01 · Follow AIO05 Type B in parallel
  • Plan cap + service degraded. Words Processed at plan cap AND user-facing service is degraded. AIO01

Weekly review

AIO02
  • Wrong rate > 10%. 2 or more "Wrong" tags in the 20-conversation sample. AIO02 · Schedule fixes per AIO03
  • PII in a conversation. Any conversation contains PII that A.C.E. produced or revealed. AIO02 · AIO05 Type C
  • Spanish canary fails. Either language detection or the $44,200 answer is wrong. AIO02 · AIO05 Type A + AIO03 Persona
  • Repeated mishandling. A topic appears repeatedly that A.C.E. consistently mishandles. AIO02
  • Spanish < 3% for 4 weeks. Four consecutive weekly checks under 3% — bilingual service underutilized. AIO02

Monthly report

AIO04
  • Not found > 15% (monthly figure). AIO04 · KB coverage gap
  • Persona Failure > 15% (monthly figure). AIO04 · Drift; review AIO03 history
  • Spanish < 3% across the month (2+ month trend). AIO04
  • Success rate < 97%. Platform or integration concern. AIO04
  • Plan cap reached. Persistent across cycles → recommend plan upsize. AIO04
  • Single non-US country > 15% of conversations. Possible bot traffic. AIO04 · Verify with IT

  • Data Source Control — broadening to "My Data + General LLM". AIO03 Section D
  • Whitelisted Domains — disables Share Link. Also notify office IT. AIO03 Section D
  • Conversation Retention / Visibility. Also notify office IT. AIO03 Section D
  • Citations enable/disable · Agent Language. AIO03 Section D
  • External or removed sources. Adding sources outside Bernalillo County government domains, or removing a source currently in use. AIO03 Section D
  • Beta Actions. Web Search, Orchestrate, Drive Conversions, Verify Responses, or Document Analyst. AIO03 Section C

Incident response

AIO05

Records & IPRA

AIO07
  • Paralegal content / attorney-client privilege. The request implicates Paralegal content that may be privileged. AIO07 B.6 · Notify before responding
  • Media, oversight body, or litigation. Request comes from media, an oversight body, or relates to actual or anticipated litigation. AIO07 B.6 · Notify before responding
  • Security exposure. Responsive records would expose system prompts or configuration in a security-relevant way. AIO07 B.6 · Notify before responding
  • Burdensome / broad route (14-2-10). A request triggers the Section 14-2-10 burdensome or broad path. Written notice in 15 days with a good-faith production plan. AIO07 B.4 + B.6

How to notify

Email both the Assessor and the Deputy on the same thread. Use a subject line that starts with the SOP ID and the severity word.

Subject: AIO05 / CRITICAL — short description

For Critical items (PII, threats, agent-down), also send a phone or chat ping in addition to the email. Don't wait for a reply before containment steps.

What the email should include

Five lines, in this order. Keep it tight — detail goes in the linked tracker or incident log.

  • What happened — one sentence.
  • When — timestamp.
  • Scope — how many users / which conversations.
  • What you've done — containment, rollback, or holding action.
  • What you need from them — acknowledge, decision, or just FYI.
Thresholds note. All AIO04 thresholds (Not found, Persona Failure, Spanish, success rate, plan cap, single-country share) apply to the monthly figure only. Daily and weekly snapshots crossing thresholds is expected sample-size volatility — not a flag. Notify on the monthly only, unless the daily-check rule for that metric independently applies.
