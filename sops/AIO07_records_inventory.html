Rule. Do not destroy any A.C.E. record without a custodian-approved disposition under the LGRRDS and 14-1-8 NMSA 1978. A.C.E. records have no dedicated LGRRDS series yet, so each mapping is a custodian decision documented here — not a unilateral one. Review this inventory annually, after every AIO03 or BIO01 change that alters what data is produced, and whenever a vendor relationship changes.
