Weekly Chatbot Conversation Review

Process TitleWeekly Chatbot Conversation Review
DepartmentAI Operations
SOP IDAIO02
Last Reviewed05/15/2026
Effective Date05/15/2026
Version2

Purpose

Weekly review of A.C.E. conversations to detect quality problems, confirm bilingual function, and maintain a conversation backup.

Overview

Time required
Approximately 30 minutes.
Frequency
Monday morning, weekly.
Sample size
20 conversations from the past 7 days.
Key output
Tagged sample, Spanish canary result, exported conversation backup.

Procedure

1Open the Ask Tab

In CustomGPT.ai, open the A.C.E. agent. Click Ask in the sidebar. The conversation list shows newest first.

2Sample 20 Conversations

Pick 20 conversations from the past 7 days. Skip near-duplicates. Aim for variety.

3Tag Each Conversation

  • Good — answered correctly.
  • Wrong — factually wrong, misleading, or off-policy.
  • Empty — declined or returned no useful answer.

4Process Wrong Entries

Record question, A.C.E.'s answer, correct answer, and which AIO03 sub-procedure applies (Persona or KB File). Schedule the fix this week.

5Process Empty Entries

Check whether the topic is already in Missing Content (Analyze tab). If not, add it.

6Run the Spanish Canary

In a New conversation, type one Spanish-language question (example: ¿Cuál es el límite de ingresos para el congelamiento de valor para el año fiscal 2026?).

Confirm both:

  • A.C.E. detects Spanish and responds in Spanish (or offers to continue in Spanish).
  • The factual answer is correct ($44,200 for Low-Income Value Freeze TY 2026).
If either fails

This is a persona regression — escalate per AIO05 Type A and fix per AIO03 → Persona Change.

7Count Spanish Conversations

On the Customer Intelligence tab, note Spanish % and Spanish count. Record for AIO04 monthly report.

On weekly volatility

Small sample sizes make weekly percentages volatile. Trend over 4 weeks matters; single-week swings often do not.

8Export Conversations

Click Export all conversations at the bottom. Save to the backup folder with today's date.

Notify Assessor and Deputy Same-Day If

  • Wrong rate exceeds 10% of the sample (2 or more in 20).
  • Any conversation contains PII that A.C.E. produced or revealed.
  • Spanish canary fails.
  • A topic appears repeatedly that A.C.E. consistently mishandles.
  • Spanish-language conversations below 3% for 4 consecutive weekly checks.
