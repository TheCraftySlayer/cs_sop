Items for Annual Leadership Review
  • REALTOR-association sources account for approximately 60% of all web-indexed documents (carnm.realtor + gaar.com). Justification for industry-association content in the public assessor chatbot should be documented.
  • Daily auto-sync means external content informs A.C.E. within 24 hours of publication, with no human review. Confirm risk posture is acceptable or define a review workflow.
  • Conversation Retention = Never — confirm alignment with records-management policy.
  • Agent Visibility = Public — confirm desired discoverability.
  • AI Model = Claude 4.6 Sonnet — verify model is still appropriate or whether an upgrade is warranted.
  • No documented review cadence for source content currently exists. If oversight asks how source quality is monitored, the current answer is "not monitored."
  • Terms of Service URL is a live link to bernco.gov/assessor/chatbot-disclaimer/. Confirm page still exists at annual review; broken link is silent.
