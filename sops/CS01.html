Purpose

This SOP outlines the standardized process for receiving, reviewing, validating, and completing Veteran’s Exemption requests to ensure accuracy, compliance with statutory requirements, and timely customer service.

Scope

This procedure applies to all staff involved in Veteran’s Exemption entry, including:

Customer Service Counter staff receiving submissions

Customer Service staff reviewing Veteran’s Exemption claims

Customer Service staff translogging completed entry

This SOP covers Veteran Exemptions entry, corrections, and removal.

Veteran’s Exemption Overview

Veterans Exemption

Benefit: Reduces the taxable value of your property by $10,000. This credit can also be used on other properties, including commercial, and the DMV.

Eligibility: Honorably discharged veteran or unmarried surviving spouse who served at least 90 consecutive days (other than training). Applies to real property or manufactured homes. Can be combined with the disabled veteran percentage 90% and lower exemption (this excludes VTW -100%). The back of the certificate will show if part or all this exemption has been used before. In cases when a remaining amount is available.

Application Process: Obtain a Certificate of Eligibility from the New Mexico Department of Veterans Services (NMDVS) by submitting their Veteran Application form. Bring the original certificate to the Assessor's Office for endorsement. Once applied, it carries over year to year without reapplication. If the Veteran moves, they must present certificate for endorsement for the new residence.

Required Documents: DD-214 (discharge papers), Driver’s License/State Issued ID, and NMDVS certificate.

Deadlines: Within 30 days of Notice of Value for current year application; apply anytime for future years.

How to Apply: Start with NMDVS (call 505-346-3986 or visit dmv.nm.gov). Then submit it to Assessor.

Disabled Veterans Exemption

Benefit:

For 100% disabled veterans, it's a full (100%) waiver of property taxes on the primary residence. This is in addition to the $10,000 standard veterans’ exemption.

The percentage-based exemption exempts a percentage of the Veteran’s primary residence taxable value equal to the Veteran’s service-connected disability rating (e.g., 50% disability = 50% exemption).

Eligibility: Veteran with a federally recognized service-connected disability (rated by U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs). Applies to primary residence, including jointly owned with spouse. Unmarried surviving spouses may qualify if the veteran died from service-connected causes.

Application Process: Apply for a Disabled Certificate of Eligibility from NMDVS, then submit to Assessor.

Required Documents: VA disability rating letter, NMDVS certificate, proof of residency.

Deadlines: Same as veterans’ exemption.

How to Apply: Via NMDVS first, then Assessor.
